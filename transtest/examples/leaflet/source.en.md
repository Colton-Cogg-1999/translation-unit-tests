# Feverol 500 mg film-coated tablets

## Package leaflet: information for the user

Read all of this leaflet carefully before you start taking this medicine.
Keep this leaflet. You may need to read it again.

## 1. What Feverol is and what it is used for

Feverol contains paracetamol, which belongs to a group of medicines called analgesics.
It is used to treat mild to moderate pain and fever in adults and adolescents weighing more than 50 kg.

## 2. What you need to know before you take Feverol

Do not take Feverol if you are allergic to paracetamol.
Talk to your doctor before taking Feverol if you have kidney problems and are over 65 years of age.
Do not take Feverol together with other medicines that contain paracetamol.
If you are taking warfarin, your doctor may need to check your blood more often.
It can increase the effect of warfarin when taken regularly for more than a week.
Avoid drinking alcohol while you are taking this medicine.
Feverol can be used during pregnancy if clinically needed, but you should use the lowest possible dose for the shortest possible time.

## 3. How to take Feverol

The recommended dose is 1 or 2 tablets every 4 to 6 hours as needed.
Do not take more than 8 tablets (4,000 mg) in 24 hours.
Leave at least 4 hours between doses.
If you have liver problems, the maximum daily dose is 6 tablets (3,000 mg).
Swallow the tablets whole with a glass of water.
Do not give Feverol to children under 12 years of age.
If your fever lasts more than 3 days or your pain lasts more than 5 days, talk to your doctor.

## 4. If you take more Feverol than you should

Contact your doctor or go to the nearest hospital immediately, even if you feel well.
This is because too much paracetamol can cause delayed, serious liver damage.

## 5. How to store Feverol

Store below 25 °C.
Do not use this medicine after the expiry date which is stated on the carton.
Keep this medicine out of the sight and reach of children.
